How Alex refinishes a floor

Five steps. None skipped.

The visit · from Alex's jobs

  1. 01

    The visit.

    Alex comes to your home, usually within 2–3 days, depending on availability. He measures with a laser and gives you your refinishing price during the visit. The written scope follows by email, ready to sign.

  2. 02

    Sand to bare wood.

    A drum sander across the room, an edger along the walls, until the old finish and the wear are gone.

  3. 03

    Smooth it out.

    A final sanding pass leaves the bare wood smooth, so the color goes on evenly.

  4. 04

    Stain, your color.

    Early American, Special Walnut, Espresso, a natural look, or the color you have in mind.

  5. 05

    Three coats.

    Bona water-based or oil-based polyurethane, three coats on every floor.

Every step is written into your scope before we start.

Questions

Straight answers.

Rather ask Alex? Call or text (475) 999-1442.

How much does it cost to refinish hardwood floors?

It depends on the square footage, the condition of the wood, the color and finish you choose, and whether the stair treads are part of the job. That's why the price comes from the visit, after Alex measures your floor, and the written scope shows exactly what's included before anything starts.

Can my floors be refinished, or do they need to be replaced?

Often they can be refinished. Many of the floors we work on are decades old. Alex checks the wood at the visit and tells you straight whether sanding will bring it back. Worn, scratched or gray is usually a refinishing job, not a new floor.

How long does it take, and when can we use the floors again?

It depends on the area, the condition of the floor and the finish you choose. Alex gives you the schedule for your floor at the visit, including when you can walk on it again and when the furniture can go back.

How does the free estimate work?

Alex comes to your home, usually within 2–3 days, depending on availability, and on your schedule. He looks at the floor and measures every room with a laser. For refinishing, you get the price during the visit. The written scope follows by email, ready to sign. No cost and no obligation.

Who will be working in my home?

Alex and his crew. Alex is the owner: he answers the phone, does the visit and the estimate, and runs every job. The person who quoted your floor is the person responsible for it.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. A Bueno Hardwood Floors LLC is a registered Connecticut Home Improvement Contractor, HIC.0705573, and carries general liability and workers' compensation insurance. Ask for the certificate of insurance before work begins.

What does the warranty cover?

Our workmanship is covered for 1 year. If the work itself fails, call or text Alex at (475) 999-1442 and he comes back to fix it. The details are in your written scope.

Do you work in my town?

Most likely, if you're in Connecticut or Westchester County, NY. Recent floors include Fairfield, Trumbull, Weston, Middlebury, Woodbury and Granby. Enter your ZIP in the form: if the home is outside our area, Alex will tell you.
